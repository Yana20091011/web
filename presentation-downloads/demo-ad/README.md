# KeyCritter classroom demo ad

**45 seconds · 1280 × 720 · H.264 MP4**

- KeyCritter-Classroom-Ad.mp4: the completed captioned ad with quiet original synthesized background music.
- KeyCritter-Ad-Silent.mp4: the same ad without audio.
- KeyCritter-Ad-Video-Slide.pptx: one widescreen slide with the video embedded and a start-on-entry playback setting. If your presentation app does not start it automatically, click Play.
- Optional-Voiceover.txt: a timed script if you want to add your own voice. No spoken narration is included in the MP4.
- Ad-Poster.png: poster frame.

## Put it in your presentation

Use the video INSTEAD of the live Demo Time section; do not also read all of the old demo instructions. This leaves roughly 3 minutes 15 seconds of a four-minute presentation for your other sections.

**Canva:** Uploads → Upload files → choose the MP4 → place it on your Demo Time slide.

**PowerPoint:** Insert → Video → This Device → choose the MP4. Or copy the supplied video slide into your deck. Save the presentation with the video embedded and test playback before class.

Download the actual video or PPTX before class. A link to the video requires Internet; the downloaded and embedded file does not.

## What is shown

The footage records the actual KeyCritter 1.10.1 website: choosing a character, selecting a background, typing, custom Enter/Drink and Backspace/Sleep reactions, saving a pet, and exporting a pet file. The key bindings are examples configured for the demo, not the default bindings. No desktop application footage is claimed; the closing card notes the available Windows app. Browser-to-desktop transfer requires export/import and is not automatic cloud synchronization.
